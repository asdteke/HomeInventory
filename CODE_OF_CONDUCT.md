# 🌟 Our Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are building HomeInventory together, and we are absolutely committed to making our community a welcoming, inspiring, and friendly place for *everyone*. 

Whether you are a seasoned open-source veteran or this is your very first pull request, no matter your background, identity, or perspective—you belong here. By participating in this project, you are agreeing to help us create a safe, respectful, and highly collaborative environment.

## 💖 Expected Behavior

We expect all members of our community to:
- **Be kind and patient:** Treat others with the same respect you'd like to receive. Let's make discussions and code reviews a positive experience.
- **Focus on the code, not the person:** Give constructive feedback about ideas and code, never about the individual.
- **Assume good intent:** Miscommunications happen! Ask clarifying questions before jumping to conclusions.
- **Share your knowledge:** Help others learn by sharing context and explaining the "why" behind your suggestions.
- **Stay open-minded:** Accept constructive feedback gracefully. We are all here to learn and improve.

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

To keep our community safe, the following behaviors will absolutely not be tolerated:
- Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, or deliberately hostile behavior.
- Publishing someone else's private information (like a physical or email address) without their explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome advances.
- Repeatedly derailing issues, pull requests, or community discussions.
- Any form of threats, intimidation, or sustained disrespectful conduct.

## 📍 Scope

This Code of Conduct applies everywhere HomeInventory happens! This includes GitHub (issues, pull requests, discussions) as well as any other community spaces or interactions directly connected to this project.

## 🤝 Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are here to help enforce this Code of Conduct. We have the right (and responsibility) to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that don't align with these values. In severe cases, we may temporarily or permanently restrict a user's participation to protect the community.

## 🗣️ Reporting an Issue

If you experience or witness behavior that makes you uncomfortable or violates this Code of Conduct, please reach out:

- **Don't escalate publicly:** Please don't start or continue a public argument, as it rarely helps fix the situation.
- **Reach out privately if a contact route is listed:** Use any maintainer or support contact method that is publicly listed in the repository, the project website, or the live application.
- **If there is no private route listed:** Open a short public issue asking for a private contact channel, without posting sensitive personal details.
- *(Note: If your report is about a software security vulnerability, please follow the steps in `SECURITY.md` instead).*

We will review reports in good faith, treat them with care, and respond as fairly and proportionately as we can.

## ⚖️ Enforcement Responses

When a violation occurs, the maintainers may respond with:
- A private, friendly warning to clarify our expectations.
- A request to change specific behavior.
- Immediate removal of the harmful content or comment.
- Temporarily locking a conversation.
- In severe or repeated cases, a permanent ban from the community.
